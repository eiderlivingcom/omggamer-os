Last updated: [omg_legal_updated]

These terms apply to the OMG PRO membership sold by [omg_legal field="name"] on OMGGamer. They add to our Terms and Conditions. Find out more about PRO on the PRO page.

1. Price and billing

  • PRO costs US$6.99 per month (or the equivalent in your selected currency, shown at checkout).
  • Your first month costs US$0.99. The introductory price is available once per person.
  • Each payment gives you one month (31 days) of PRO. If automatic renewal is shown at checkout, your membership renews every month at the regular price, charged to the payment method you used to join, until you cancel. Otherwise it ends after the month and you can renew it at any time.
  • We will tell you in advance by email if the monthly price changes. The new price applies from the next renewal after the notice; you can cancel before then.

2. Benefits

  • Member prices: an extra discount on eligible products, shown on the product page as the PRO price.
  • Other benefits are listed on the PRO page and may change over time. We will not remove the member-price benefit during a period you have already paid for.
  • PRO prices are never below our minimum price for a product, so the PRO discount can be smaller on some low-margin items.

3. Discount codes

Discount codes cannot be combined with PRO. If your cart contains a PRO membership, or you are an active PRO member, the discount code field is not available and codes already added are removed. PRO member prices apply instead.

4. Cancelling

  • You can cancel automatic renewal at any time by emailing [omg_legal field="email"] (or in My account, where available). Your membership stays active until the end of the month you have paid for and is not renewed after that.
  • Right of withdrawal: if you cancel within 14 days of first joining and have not bought anything at a PRO price, we refund that payment in full. If you have already used PRO prices, we may deduct the PRO savings you received from the refund.
  • Payments for past months are not refundable, except as required by law.

5. Fair use

PRO is for personal use. Member prices may not be used to buy products for resale. We may end a membership that is shared between people or used for reselling, and refund the unused part of the current month.
