Part 1: Digital Services Act

Single point of contact

Authorities of EU member states, the European Commission, the European Board for Digital Services and users of the Store can contact us directly at [omg_legal field="email"]. We communicate in English. Please write "DSA" in the subject line.

Reporting illegal content

If you believe a product listing, review or other content on the Store is illegal (for example counterfeit products, infringement of intellectual property rights, or unlawful statements), email [omg_legal field="email"] with:

  • a clear explanation of why you think the content is illegal;
  • the exact web address (URL) of the content;
  • your name and email address (not needed for reports about child sexual abuse material);
  • a statement that you believe in good faith that the report is accurate and complete.

We confirm receipt, review the report carefully and without undue delay, and tell you what we decided and how you can challenge the decision.

Our decisions and how to challenge them

If we remove or restrict content you posted, or suspend your account, we tell you the reasons. You can challenge our decision free of charge within six months by replying to our message or writing to [omg_legal field="email"]. A person, not only an automated system, reviews every challenge. You can also use a certified out-of-court dispute settlement body in the EU, or go to court.

Content moderation

Product listings on the Store are created by us. Customer reviews are checked against our Terms and Conditions and may be removed if they contain illegal content, spam, personal data of others or abuse. We may suspend accounts that repeatedly post clearly illegal content or repeatedly send clearly unfounded reports.

Recommendations and advertising

Product rankings and recommendations (for example "Related products" or the order of search results) are based on relevance to your search or the page you are viewing, popularity, price, discount and availability. We do not show third-party advertising on the Store. Sponsored or promoted placements, if we introduce them, will be clearly labelled.

Platform information

OMGGamer sells products directly; it is not a marketplace for third-party sellers. OMGGamer is not a very large online platform under the DSA.

Part 2: General Product Safety Regulation

The GPSR applies to physical consumer products we sell in the EU, such as gaming hardware and accessories. It does not apply to digital keys, codes or accounts.

  • Product information: for each physical product, the product page shows the manufacturer's name and contact details, the person responsible in the EU where the manufacturer is outside the EU, product identifiers (such as model or batch number), and safety warnings and instructions in the language of the country where it is sold, where these apply.
