2. Why we use it and our legal bases

  • To deliver your order and run your account: processing payments, delivering codes, shipping physical products, showing order history (performance of a contract).
  • To show the right products and prices: using your country to show prices in your currency, activation-region warnings and available payment methods (legitimate interests and contract).
  • To prevent fraud and keep the Store secure: checking orders for fraud, bot protection on sign-in and checkout, logging security events (legitimate interests and legal obligations).
  • To provide support and handle refunds, including checking codes with our partners (contract and legitimate interests).
  • To send service emails such as order confirmations, delivery emails and important account notices (contract).
  • To send marketing, such as deals and restock alerts, only if you subscribed or, where the law allows, about similar products you bought. You can unsubscribe in every email (consent or legitimate interests).
  • To meet legal duties, such as tax, accounting and consumer-protection obligations (legal obligation).
  • To improve the Store using aggregated information about how it is used (legitimate interests).

We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you. Fraud checks may flag an order for manual review by a person.

3. Who we share it with

  • Payment providers, to process payments and refunds and to prevent fraud.
  • Hosting, security and infrastructure providers that run our servers, content delivery, backups and bot protection (for example Cloudflare Turnstile on sign-in forms).
  • Email providers that send order and marketing emails for us.
  • Google, only if you choose "Continue with Google" to sign in.
  • Product partners and distributors from whom we buy the products you order. We send them only what they need to supply the product, normally our own order reference and not your name or email. For physical products, couriers receive your delivery details.
  • Professional advisers and authorities, such as accountants, lawyers, tax authorities, courts or police, when needed or required by law.
  • A buyer of our business, if we sell or merge the business, under this policy.

Our service providers act on our instructions under data processing agreements.

4. International transfers

We are based in India and our providers may process data in other countries, including the EU, the UK and the United States. When we transfer personal data from the EU or UK to a country without an adequacy decision, we use safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. Ask us for a copy at [omg_legal field="email"].

5. How long we keep it

  • Account data: while your account is open, and up to 2 years after your last activity if you do not close it.
  • Orders, invoices and payment records: as long as tax and accounting law requires (normally 8 years).
  • Support messages: up to 3 years after the case is closed.
  • Security logs: up to 12 months.
  • Marketing data: until you unsubscribe, then only the record needed to respect your choice.

6. Security

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS), encrypted storage of passwords and delivered codes, access controls, two-factor sign-in for staff tools and regular backups. No system is completely secure, so please use a strong, unique password and keep your codes private.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and get a copy;
  • correct inaccurate data or complete incomplete data;
  • delete your data (we keep what we must keep by law, such as invoices);
  • restrict or object to processing, including objecting at any time to direct marketing;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting earlier processing;
  • nominate another person to exercise your rights if you die or become unable to (India);
  • complain to a data protection authority, such as the authority in your EU country, the UK Information Commissioner's Office or the Data Protection Board of India.

Email [omg_legal field="email"] to use your rights. We reply within one month and may ask you to confirm your identity. Grievances about personal data can also be sent to our Grievance Officer: [omg_legal field="grievance"], [omg_legal field="address_inline"].
