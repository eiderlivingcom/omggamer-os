Cookie Policy

Last updated: [omg_legal_updated]

Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser. This policy lists the cookies OMGGamer, operated by [omg_legal field="name"], uses and why. For how we handle personal data in general, see our Privacy Policy.

1. Cookies we use

We currently use only cookies that are needed to run the Store or to remember choices you make. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics cookies. If we add any in the future, we will ask for your consent first and update this page.

Our bot protection on sign-in and checkout forms (Cloudflare Turnstile), where enabled, may read or set technical data needed to tell people from bots. Payment providers may set their own cookies on their payment pages to prevent fraud.

2. Managing cookies

Necessary cookies do not need consent because the Store cannot work without them. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, but signing in, the cart and checkout will not work without necessary cookies. Deleting the preference cookies resets your country and currency to the ones we detect automatically.
