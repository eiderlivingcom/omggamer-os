OMG Buyer Protection

Last updated: [omg_legal_updated]

Every order on OMGGamer is covered by OMG Buyer Protection at no extra cost. If something goes wrong with your order, we fix it: a working replacement or your money back.

What is covered

  • Codes that do not work: invalid, already redeemed or rejected by the platform.
  • Not as described: a different platform, region, edition or language than the product page stated.
  • Not delivered: the product did not arrive within the delivery time shown.
  • Accounts: login details that do not work or an account that is not as described.
  • Physical products: items that arrive damaged, faulty or are not what you ordered.

How long you are covered

Report a problem within 30 days of delivery. For pre-orders, the 30 days start on the release date.

How to make a claim

  1. Email [omg_legal field="email"] with your order number (you find it in My account → Orders and in your order email).
  2. Describe the problem and, for codes, attach a screenshot of the activation error.
  3. We check the order with our partner, usually within 1–3 working days, and send you a replacement or a refund, whichever you prefer.

What is not covered

  • Changing your mind after a digital product has been delivered.
  • Products that match their description but do not work in your region or on your device.
  • Content blocked because of a VPN, a region change or a breach of the platform's rules.
  • Codes that were activated successfully, including on the wrong account.

Your statutory rights

OMG Buyer Protection is in addition to your legal rights as a consumer, not instead of them. See our Refund Policy and Terms and Conditions for the full rules.
