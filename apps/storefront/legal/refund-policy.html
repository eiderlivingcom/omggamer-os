This policy explains when you can get a refund or replacement from OMGGamer, operated by [omg_legal field="name"]. It is part of our Terms and Conditions and does not limit your statutory rights.

1. Digital products (keys, codes, gift cards, accounts, top-ups)

When we refund or replace

Contact us within 30 days of delivery and we will replace the product or refund you in full if:

  • the code is invalid, already used or rejected by the platform;
  • the product is for a different platform, region, edition or language than the product page stated;
  • you did not receive the product within the delivery time shown;
  • account details do not work or the account is not as described;
  • you were charged twice or charged the wrong amount.

When we cannot refund

  • You changed your mind after the code or account details were delivered. At checkout you ask us to deliver straight away and confirm that your right of withdrawal ends once delivery starts.
  • The code was activated successfully, including on the wrong account.
  • The product matched the product page, but it does not work in your region, on your device or with your platform account (please check the activation region and system requirements before buying).
  • The platform blocked the content because of a VPN, a region change or a breach of the platform's rules.

What we need from you

Your order number and, where possible, a screenshot of the activation error with the date visible. We check the code with our partner, which usually takes 1–3 working days. If the check confirms the problem, we send a working replacement or a refund, whichever you prefer.

Orders not yet delivered

If delivery has not started (for example a pre-order or an order held for a security check), you can cancel and get a full refund.

2. Physical products (hardware and accessories)

  • 14-day right of withdrawal: you can return a physical product within 14 days of receiving it without giving a reason. Tell us at [omg_legal field="email"] first so we can give you the return address. Send the product back within 14 days of telling us.
  • Return it unused, complete and in its original packaging where possible. You may handle it as you would in a shop; if you use it beyond that, we may reduce the refund to reflect the loss in value.
  • Unless the product is faulty or we sent the wrong item, you pay the return postage.
  • We refund the product price and the standard delivery cost within 14 days of receiving the return (or proof you sent it), to your original payment method.
  • Faulty or damaged products: tell us within 30 days of delivery and we will repair, replace or refund at no cost to you. Your statutory warranty rights also apply.

3. OMG PRO membership

You can cancel PRO at any time; it stays active until the end of the period you paid for. If you cancel within 14 days of first joining and have not used any PRO benefit, we refund that payment. Details are in the PRO Membership Terms.

4. How refunds are paid

  • Refunds go back to the original payment method, in the currency you paid. We do not charge a fee for refunds; your bank may apply its own exchange rate.
  • Card refunds normally appear within 5–10 working days after we issue them.
  • Instead of a refund you may choose a replacement product of the same value where available.

5. Chargebacks

Please contact us before opening a chargeback; we can usually help faster. If a chargeback is opened on a delivered order, we may suspend the account while the case is open and ask the platform to deactivate the delivered code.
